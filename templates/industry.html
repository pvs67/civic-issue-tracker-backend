Industry & CSR Collaboration Portal

Enabling industries, startups, MSMEs, CSR organisations and research institutions to mentor, fund, and support prototyping of matched civic challenges.

◆

Mentorship

Industry experts guide student-faculty teams through technical and business validation of proposed solutions.

◆

Funding & CSR Support

Organisations can fund prototyping and pilot deployment for challenges matched to their CSR focus areas.

◆

Prototyping & Deployment

Access to labs, manufacturing partners and testing infrastructure to move solutions from prototype to field deployment.

Full industry onboarding and matching workflow is planned as a future enhancement beyond this prototype phase. This module currently outlines the intended collaboration model as per the platform's technical approach.
