Portal at a Glance

How It Works

Report, analyse, match, implement and monitor — the MVP flow from the technical approach.

1

Citizen Report

Submit a challenge with description, photograph and map location. Any citizen can raise an issue in minutes.

2

AI-Enabled Triage

The engine assigns category and priority, extracts keywords, prepares a summary and flags possible duplicate reports.

3

Collaborative Solution

Matched university teams build prototypes with industry mentoring, funding and implementation support.
